## B23 · Lector 82 / trabajo 76 · La colina del cumpleaños

**Reemplazo:** `borradores/cap-76-where-the-house-was.md:147`–165. Antes quedan el calcetín, la mano retirada y «Yes. It’s in writing»; después Harry se queda con el calcetín y reconoce el gesto del brazo. Es el beso de despedida existente, con un desarrollo medio, no una reconciliación nueva.

La base ya dice en 147 «She looked at his mouth, and the split in his lip». Por tanto, la afirmación de que no hay ninguna mirada a la boca como anticipo en el manuscrito necesita esta corrección. La mirada y el beso son anteriores a esta muestra; no los inventa el laboratorio.

<!-- INICIO B23 -->

She walked up to him with her left arm behind her back. He pushed his hands down into his pockets until his shoulders hurt. Up close he could see the fine tremor going through her mouth, and the small bright line of sweat at her hair, and he could not think of one thing he was allowed to do about either.

Her eyes went to his mouth, to the split in his lip. She caught his collar with her right hand and rose on her toes. Her breath caught. He bent towards her before she had finished pulling.

Her lips were very cold. He felt them shake against his, felt the uneven pressure as she tried to stay high enough, and went lower with her. The split in his lip stung. He shut his eyes and kissed her back, holding the sock inside his pocket so tightly that his nails went into his palm.

She had stopped trying to pull him nearer. Her fingers rested at his collar. For a few seconds he could feel her mouth answer his, quite clearly, and he knew that she was there; he had come through all that dark, and she had come across the hill, and he could not get his hands out even to hold the edge of her cloak.

She lowered her heels. Their mouths parted. He opened his eyes, still bent towards her, and her fingers were already smoothing his collar down.

“That was the last one,” said Lyra.

He had no breath for it. It came out anyway, because he knew the answer to that, he had always known it.

“This year.”

He waited. The first light was full on her face. Her lips moved a little, and he stared at them, ready for the two words, for *It’s August*, for anything at all in that voice.

She pressed her lips together.

Then she turned and walked back along the ridge. At the broom she stopped, with her back to him. Her shoulders rose and came down. She put a leg across and took the handle in her right hand, with the left still close against her body. When she was steady in the air she let go, and her right hand crossed and closed round her own left arm, just above the wrist, and pressed. She flew like that until she was too small to see.

**Enlace y comparación:** el «No» anterior a que él coja la muñeca permanece intacto; un beso iniciado por ella no lo revoca. Se conserva el fracaso de la réplica ritual. El calcetín actúa como algo que él tiene en la mano dentro del bolsillo, en vez de añadir otra explicación sobre cuánto quiere tocarla. No hay promesa de seguridad ni una respuesta sobre la casa.

## B24 · Lector 91 / trabajo 85 · Bacon

**Reemplazo:** `borradores/cap-85-its-a-nice-one.md:73`–79. Antes queda «Bacon», dicho para él; después el separador y el domingo. Versión breve: la diferencia respecto a la variante D del laboratorio anterior es que se concreta el beso sin convertirlo en una estancia.

<!-- INICIO B24 -->

She saw it reach him. His eyes filled, slowly, from the bottom, and his mouth opened. *Don’t speak*, she thought. *Not one word.*

He shut it. His hands went deeper into his pockets.

She went up the two steps, caught the edge of his collar between a grey woollen finger and thumb, and drew him down to meet her. His lips were chapped and cold. She put her mouth against his, felt him tremble, and held the collar just long enough for him to turn his head a little and kiss her back.

It was a small one. She had kept her eyes shut for scarcely a breath. When she opened them his face was still close, his mouth unsteady, and neither of them had made a sound. There had been nothing to hear.

She smoothed his collar once and went past him, up the stairs. At the turn she had her left hand flat against her ribs and a smile on her face that she could feel returning, stiffly, while her boots went on making wet marks on the stone.

**Enlace y comparación:** conserva los escalones, el guante, el contacto breve y el silencio material que protege la escena. La contestación es un cambio de posición de su boca, no una declaración nueva ni unas manos que la sujetan. El frasco no se convierte en pago por el beso. Tampoco se sustituye por éste el buen día de las amigas: ese día sucede antes y tiene sus propios deseos y escenas.
