# Traducción inglesa oficial — coreano 61: Dream

Saturday morning, herald of a golden weekend. Harry had slept in and made his way to the Great Hall a step behind everyone else. He walked in mid-yawn to find Ron stabbing at a roasted potato with his fork, who called out the moment he spotted him.

"Harry! What took you so long?"

"Bad dreams. I thought I'd sleep like a log after yesterday—"

Harry scrunched his face as though the memory alone was unpleasant.

"Strange dreams?"

"Yeah. I was a snake, crawling through the corridors of the Ministry. Kept slithering toward some closed door at the far end and failing to get through."

"What?"

Ron and Hermione both startled and dropped their forks.

They had spent the summer with Harry and heard enough to know what his recurring dreams meant. Those dreams were real—whenever Voldemort's emotions surged, his memories bled back through the scar, flooding Harry's mind with visions.

"That's definitely him—!"

"Yeah. Must be. He's up to something."

Harry replied with studied indifference and reached for the pile of roasted potatoes on the platter. They were still steaming despite the late hour—a Preservation Charm, by the look of it. He speared a piece, dragged it through a generous dollop of sour cream, and popped it into his mouth, chewing as he spoke.

"Come to think of it, I've been having this dream for a few days now. I can't remember it clearly, but he seems to be after something down in the Ministry's lower levels. What on earth is hidden in the basement of the Ministry?"

"A secret door beneath the Ministry. Now that's intriguing."

Ron mulled it over as he loaded his plate with a well-browned sausage and a boiled egg.

"Maybe that's where they keep their brains. Especially Percy—the only explanation for how he behaves is that his brain's been sitting in a tank somewhere."

Ron's joke lifted Harry's mood a fraction. He snickered.

"Plausible, but I'm not sure anyone would bother stealing it."

"Useful as a weapon, though. Get close enough to Percy's brain and you'd go mad from the nonstop lecturing."

"Ron. Harry. This is hardly the time for jokes."

Hermione gave Ron a mild look of reproach, then turned to Harry.

"Harry. Tell Professor Dumbledore. Now."

"I will. Later."

"Harry, I mean right now! If he's after something, every moment counts!"

"That won't be necessary."

Harry wiped the sauce from his fingers with a napkin, then helped himself to a sausage the same way Ron had, chewing steadily. He'd been losing so much blood lately that meat had never tasted so necessary.

"This dream has been going on for weeks. What I've felt through it is deep frustration. He clearly hasn't managed to get inside yet. Whatever's in there, it's somewhere even he can't easily reach—"

"Harry, what happened to your hand?"

Hermione pointed. Harry turned his hand over, examining the scar.

"Oh, this? Umbridge has been calling me in every evening and carving into me with something sharp."

"What?"

Harry swallowed the last of his sausage and told them everything—what the 'extra study sessions' in Umbridge's office actually were, and how they were conducted.

He left out the part about Carina, of course. That was too private, too singular a thing to share.

Ron and Hermione sat with their mouths open when he finished. Before either could speak, Ginny, who had crept closer during the telling, erupted.

"That's torture! Harry, go to Dumbledore right now!"

"I should, yes—but I'm not sure what he can do about it. She has the Ministry's protection."

"What does that matter? You have scars on the back of your hand!"

"Professor Dumbledore isn't one to bend rules or defy authority. Not that he can't—it's more that he prefers not to, if he can help it."

Ginny knew this well enough to hold back any real criticism, though she grumbled all the same.

"But why? Couldn't Professor Dumbledore just—fix this if he wanted to?"

"He has his reasons, I'm sure."

Harry thought back to the fake Moody incident the previous year. Dumbledore had known Moody wasn't himself, and yet he hadn't stopped Harry or Sirius. It wasn't indifference—it felt more like a deliberate choice to watch and wait, to see what Harry would do on his own.

Perhaps Dumbledore was afraid of changing people. Of intervening too soon.

"Either way, he's busy. He mentioned finding another lead recently, so I'll have to wait on that—"

As Harry murmured this, he caught Draco's gaze from the Slytherin table again. This time it was blatant enough that Draco didn't look away when their eyes met. His searching stare swept slowly over Harry and the space around him.

He clearly wanted something. Harry considered it for a moment, then gave a small nod and rose from his seat.

"It's the weekend, so I should catch Professor Dumbledore before he leaves on his trip. I'll stop by the headmaster's office and then head back to the dormitory."

"Don't dawdle. Information is best exchanged quickly!"

"I know."

Harry grabbed another handful of sausages, feeding them into his mouth one by one as he left the Great Hall.

He passed through the long corridor leading away from the hall, climbed the moving staircases, and arrived at a wide, open landing with nowhere to hide. He spun around.

Across the way, Draco was throwing himself behind a pillar with frantic urgency. He seemed to think he was hidden, but if that had been the plan, he should have left the cloak behind. One shoulder and the trailing hem of his robes were plainly visible.

"Who's there!"

Harry whipped out his wand and called out. Draco flinched.
