There are limits to what one person can do.

Carina had kept The Quibbler hidden, but something that conspicuous could not escape Umbridge's notice forever. Umbridge might have been foolish and drunk on ambition, but she had sharp instincts. The moment she laid eyes on The Quibbler, she contacted Minister for Magic Cornelius Fudge.

"This is a direct challenge to the Ministry!"

As expected, Minister Fudge erupted.

"Dumbledore has gone too far! Because the Ministry won't entertain his absurd claims, he resorts to this kind of propaganda campaign! Dolores, I am giving you a very important order!"

"Please, speak, Minister!"

"There's no question this was produced at Hogwarts. The people behind this magazine have violated the Ministry's publication ban! That means they have broken the law! I'll assign you several Aurors—arrest those responsible and bring them to the Ministry!"

Fudge's order was precisely what Umbridge had been hoping for. Commanding Aurors and making arrests was a privilege reserved for senior officials—Ministers, Heads of the Auror Office. This was a chance to wield that kind of power. If she succeeded, she would be one step closer to something far greater.

Lost in that rosy vision, Umbridge suddenly thought of Dumbledore and shrank.

Whenever Dumbledore crossed her mind, her body trembled. That moment—when she had been about to use a curse on a student—the crushing force that had seized her entire body. No wand raised, no incantation spoken, and yet with the barest movement of his fingertips, Dumbledore had pinned her in mid-air like a specimen.

Umbridge was acutely sensitive to power, but even she could not see the bottom of Dumbledore. The memory of that day was branded into her like a scar.

"...But what do we do about Dumbledore? As long as that old man is standing guard, arresting the students will be nearly impossible."

Minister Fudge groaned at the thought of Dumbledore as well. Having worked alongside him for so many years, Fudge understood Dumbledore's power better than almost anyone.

...And yet, even now, the assumption that Dumbledore would not cross a certain line—was that faith in the power of office, or faith in the man's character?

If pressed for an answer, one would have to conclude that Fudge simply hadn't thought about it very deeply.

"Dumbledore... yes, he won't stand by and watch Hogwarts students get arrested."

The greatest wizard alive—the man who had brought Gellert Grindelwald to his knees and stood against Voldemort himself.

To think that one or two Aurors could arrest Dumbledore was no different from thinking one or two Aurors could bring down Voldemort. Even Fudge's boundless optimism couldn't conjure that picture.

In the end, Fudge hit upon a scheme.

"I'll lure him out myself. If I summon him on a matter of his Wizengamot standing, he'll have no choice but to appear."

Once the plan took shape, more ideas came flooding in. He was seized by the feeling that he had become a great strategist, and he kept producing schemes.

"And we'll set up a Floo Network connection between Hogwarts and the Ministry! The moment Dumbledore leaves, you take the Aurors straight in and make the arrests!"

It was a perfect plan. Fudge rose from his chair with a thrill of satisfaction. If everything went accordingly, he would have Dumbledore and Harry Potter cornered, and peace and stability would be restored.

There were minor complications, but once Dumbledore and Harry Potter were dealt with, normal life could resume. Without them in the way, he could dispatch Aurors to apprehend Karkaroff and the escaped prisoners.

It all seemed so simple that he almost regretted not having done it sooner.

"And then it's over! Victory!"

Optimism swallowed reality whole. Minister Fudge kept his eyes open but chose to be blind. What he saw with them frightened him more than the darkness behind closed lids, and so he chose foolishness.

They worked out the finer details. With each addition, the scheme grew more specific and more sinister. Power is dangerous precisely because it can transform even the most absurd delusions into reality. Their imaginations began to push further and further past any reasonable limit.

Then, in the midst of it all, Fudge recalled an article he had seen in The Quibbler and asked, almost as an afterthought—

"Ah, speaking of which—there was something in that article, about Professor Snape and—"

He got no further. One look at Umbridge's face, which appeared on the verge of exploding with fury, and Fudge fell silent.

It was clear that even Umbridge found Snape's appearance less than welcome.

[Carina. There is something I need you to do.

The Ministry is going to lure Dumbledore out. In that window, Aurors will infiltrate Hogwarts through the Floo Network and attempt to arrest Harry Potter.

At that moment—if our plan holds—Harry Potter will instead use the Floo Network to enter the Ministry himself.

You are to help him.

I understand it may seem strange to be asked to assist Harry Potter. But it is part of a larger design, so follow it as instructed.

This will be the last thing I ask of you. Once this is done successfully, we will have restored His trust.

When that time comes, you need not be afraid any longer. You—we—will hold the most glorious position of all.

Good luck. Your Father.]

Carina raised her wand with trembling hands.

A letter from her father, carrying a hidden message.

She had burned dozens of letters just like it, but none had ever been this hard to destroy.

The tip of her wand swayed like a reed in the wind. The letter was not small, but pointing her wand at it felt nearly impossible.

"*Incendio.*"

After three failed attempts, she finally managed to press the tip of her wand to the paper. The flame that leapt from it consumed the letter whole. As though it had been born only to burn, the paper vanished in an instant.

Acrid smoke clung to her face. Carina could not bring herself to open her eyes, and she sank to the floor where she stood.

"I can't... I can't do this..."

Her father would have laid a trap at the Ministry. Other Death Eaters would be waiting for Harry. The moment Harry Potter set foot in that place... he would die.
Even the most incurable optimist could not convince themselves Harry would survive. Broderick Bode had died. There was no reason to believe Harry Potter would fare any better.

Which meant Carina had to stop Harry Potter from reaching the Ministry in the first place.

"It's all right. If I do this..."

No one would suspect her if Harry Potter simply failed to arrive. They would only know the plan had gone wrong.

Then her father's scheme would come to nothing, and that would be the end of it. No one would have to die.

Even if this was only a delay, Carina could not bring herself to accept someone's death.

'Umbridge's Ideal Type Revealed... Hooked Nose, Cold Eyes. Could It Be?'

The moment anyone at Hogwarts read that headline, they knew immediately who had published The Quibbler. No one else had the nerve to humiliate Snape quite like that—no one except Harry Potter.

The fact that Harry had spent the following day actively avoiding Snape only confirmed it. As did the fact that Snape had spent the same day actively hunting for Harry.

Snape abruptly abandoned the regular curriculum and ordered his students to brew a potion that would leave anyone who drank it gravely ill. It was obvious he intended to test its toxicity on Harry at the first available opportunity.

In any case, entirely apart from that, the members of the D.A. were in a festive mood.

"...*Dolores Umbridge is a short woman with a slightly blunt nose and eyes that glitter like a beetle's. She consoled herself by insisting she looked young for her age, but deep down she harbored a complex about her appearance. Then, at Hogwarts, she met her destiny. A kind yet stern middle-aged man who seemed to make her every flaw disappear... His name was...*"

It was the sort of content that would have given Snape every right to come after them with a vial of poison. But as far as the students who despised Snape were concerned, his fury was simply another source of entertainment.

"Oh, Severus Snape—a man with eyes cold as the void!"

George read the line aloud, and the room erupted. Several students laughed so hard they cried. If there was ever such a thing as tears of genuine emotion, surely this was it.

The students crowded around the members of Diggory's Arm and showered them with praise.

"It suited them so perfectly I actually believed it for a second!"

"Umbridge, who only favors Slytherins, and Snape, who despises everyone who isn't a Slytherin. What would their child turn out like?"

"A child of those two would be anything but pure! They're the complete opposite of each other!"

The students dissolved into laughter. Not a trace of anxiety showed on any of their faces. To them, even The Quibbler was just another form of entertainment.

Hermione, however, understood the gravity of what they had done better than anyone.

"Harry. You do realize what we did is illegal. We have no idea how Umbridge and Minister Fudge are going to respond."

Publishing the magazine had been an open act of defiance against the Ministry and Umbridge. It might be dismissed as a student prank, but given that Fudge had already dragged Harry before a tribunal, it was unlikely he would let this slide.

The Quibbler had already spread beyond Hogwarts to the wider wizarding community, and people were now raising serious questions about Igor Karkaroff and Voldemort. From Fudge's perspective, it was a thorn he would want pulled out immediately.

Against Hermione's concern, Harry shrugged.

"There's no proof I wrote it."

"Harry. They're not stupid. You think they won't suspect you the moment they see an article about Umbridge and Snape's—pfft—*scandal*?"

Even Hermione, mid-worry, had to bite her lip to keep from laughing. Harry laughed out loud.

"What can they do with suspicion? All the evidence is in the Room of Requirement. Unless they know exactly what we went in there for, they'll never find it."

"They can get the evidence straight from your mouth! What if they use Veritaserum? Or another truth potion? What if they bribe someone, or use torture? There are plenty of ways to find out!"

"None of those will work as long as Professor Dumbledore is here."

"He can't protect us every single moment! You always have to be careful, Harry! What I mean is—don't ever let yourself be alone with her!"

At Hermione's words, Harry scratched his head and was about to nod and say he understood.

But the moment he lowered his head, a pain like a knife dragged across his scar tore through him, and he collapsed. As his eyes fell shut, the worried faces of Ron and Hermione flickered at the edges of his vision.

Then everything went dark.

Harry had experienced something like this before—while sleeping at night, or when that man's emotions ran high. Thoughts and feelings would bleed through Harry's scar from the other side.

This time was no different. Harry was standing alone in a dark room. Behind him, Lucius Malfoy knelt on one knee and spoke.

"My Lord... however, based on what we learned through Broderick Bode, the prophecy can only be retrieved by the person it concerns..."

Voldemort answered in a cold, flat voice.

"So it means I must go myself. To that place, crawling with Ministry fools who refuse to believe in my return."

"I am deeply sorry..."

"I am not blaming you, Lucius. There are limits to what an ordinary person can accomplish... This, it seems, is yours."

He conveyed his disappointment with a cold expression—as though he had measured the extent of Lucius's usefulness and drawn a line around it.

Lucius looked up at him with desperate eyes.

"B-but, my Lord! I have information!"

"...Information."

Voldemort's voice was low and cold. He tilted his chin as if to say, go on then, with the air of a man not addressing a subordinate but a tool that had failed to prove its worth.

"The Minister for Magic has been arranging something recently. He intends to lure Dumbledore out himself, and in that window, send Aurors to storm Hogwarts!"

"...And?"
"At that moment, the Ministry will be nearly empty. All the regular staff will have gone home, and the personnel who should be guarding the Ministry will have gone to Hogwarts. If you were to go then, my Lord, you could obtain the prophecy without anyone knowing."

"You're suggesting I sneak in?"

Voldemort's expression darkened with displeasure. But Lucius failed to read it.

In truth, anyone would have struggled to read his face. It had long since ceased to resemble anything ordinary.

Lucius raised his head with a proud smile.

"Yes! If you were to infiltrate while the bulk of the Ministry is away, you could reach the Department of Mysteries without being seen by a single—*gkh!*"

Lucius Malfoy's words cut off as his hands flew to his throat. An invisible force closed around his neck. That immeasurable power seized him and lifted him into the air.

Voldemort raised his wand and spoke.

"...I am fear itself. I am dread. What does dread have to fear, that it should hide? Lucius. Do you believe that I—*I*—have been silent all this time because I am afraid of the Ministry?"

"*Kgh*—n-no. I'm s-sorry—"

"Should I creep in like a rat under cover of darkness? I gave you all a task. You were to carry it out. And the result is... that I must go and fetch it myself. Laughable. If I had intended to do it myself, I would never have given the order in the first place."

Voldemort lowered his wand, and Lucius dropped to the ground. He gasped for breath, his face contorted with pain.

"...It does not matter if the Ministry fools learn of my return. Now that I know it is beyond your capabilities, I will go in person. Tonight, I will remove every obstacle in my path and take the prophecy. This is not a plan. It is a certainty. It is my own prophecy."

Voldemort declared it with cold arrogance, looking down at Lucius's bowed head.

At that moment, Harry woke.

He gasped for breath and pressed his hand hard against his scar, which burned an angry red. Ron and Hermione were beside him, watching him with anxious faces.

"Harry, what happened?"

Harry murmured, his expression dazed.

"He's... heading to the Department of Mysteries."

"What?"

"The prophecy... only the person it concerns can retrieve it. When he found that out, he was furious. Because Lucius had been handling it all this time, and in the end, he has to go himself."

Harry spoke quickly. Ron and Hermione listened to the information he was firing at them, their faces confused.

"How do you know that? Was it your scar again?"

"Yes. His memories and thoughts came through."

"Is that... real?"

"Whether it's real or not, it's definitely an opportunity."

The scar showed him Voldemort in the present moment. Which meant Voldemort had already set out, or was about to.

If that was the case... now was the chance to alert the Ministry to Voldemort's return. Even those fools couldn't ignore evidence of an intruder in their own building. It would force them to reconsider.

Harry leapt to his feet.

"We have to tell Professor Dumbledore!"

He left Ron and Hermione behind and ran straight for the headmaster's office.

The entrance to the office was guarded as always by two stone serpents. Harry stopped before them and called out the password.

"Cherry pie!"

The serpents sprang aside. Harry pushed through the door and climbed the spiral staircase. He knocked briefly and threw open the door to the headmaster's office.

Dumbledore must have stepped out for a moment—the office was empty. Harry considered waiting, then remembered the Marauder's Map he had left in the Room of Requirement.

Better to go looking than to wait indefinitely.

He ran back down the spiral staircase and headed for the Room of Requirement. As he walked the corridor after opening the door, he came face to face with Professor McGonagall.

"Potter. What brings you here?"

"I went to see Professor Dumbledore. There's something urgent I need to tell him—do you know where he is?"

"The Professor had important business outside today."

"Outside? Important business?"

"It seems Minister Fudge summoned him over some matter regarding his Wizengamot standing. Whatever he's trying to torment the Headmaster with this time..."

Professor McGonagall sighed. Harry's mind went back to Voldemort's memory—to Lucius Malfoy's words. Lucius had said the Minister for Magic intended to lure Dumbledore out, and that Aurors would storm Hogwarts in the gap.

What if Dumbledore's absence right now—called away on Wizengamot business—was exactly that?

Had the Ministry's plan already begun?

"Harry! Professor McGonagall!"

Just then, Ron came sprinting toward them, breathless. He didn't stop to catch his breath before shouting urgently—

"Aurors! Aurors! Umbridge has led Aurors in—they've just stormed the castle!"
