The third task was drawing near. The Ministry of Magic was doing everything in its power to make it the crowning jewel of their achievements.

Word had it they'd imported a rare Sphinx, constructed traps modelled after pyramids, and painstakingly selected creatures that were extraordinarily rare yet not quite lethal enough to fill the maze.

Everyone was buzzing with excitement.

Carina alone could not enjoy a single moment of it.

The rain hammered down on the lake in heavy sheets. Thunder and lightning tore through the sky and struck the earth. Every time the wind surged, the mast let out a long, groaning creak.

Aboard the Durmstrang ship, Carina was listening carefully to what the man across from her had to say.

"...Miss Malfoy. The time has come to make a choice."

Igor Karkaroff. Once a Death Eater, he had narrowly escaped Azkaban by selling out his comrades, and now he trembled with fear—more acutely sensitive to this particular threat than anyone else alive.

He looked utterly haggard, and yet his nerves were wound tighter than she had ever seen them.

"There are rumours that Bertha Jorkins, a Ministry employee, has gone missing. Are you aware of this?"

Carina opened her mouth slowly.

"...Only what I caught briefly in the Daily Prophet. It seems she still hasn't been found. If she had been, they'd have run a front-page story telling everyone to rest easy."

"Yes... the Ministry of this country seems determined to keep that fact buried... I found that suspicious, so I used my own contacts to gather news from outside Britain."

A low, snickering laugh escaped him. He spoke in a state of dishevelment and frailty that seemed impossible to reconcile with the headmaster of a wizarding school.

"Albania. Bertha Jorkins apparently made it there in one piece. But after that, she vanished without a trace. Albania... Albania. A quiet country, steeped in ancient magic. Quite ideal for a wizard in hiding... Bertha Jorkins must have encountered someone there. The last place she was sighted was a Muggle tavern..."

Slowly, the truth one does not wish to know approaches in a way that cannot be resisted. Once you begin to sense it, the horizon beyond is already black with storm clouds as far as the eye can see.

"That place also happens to be the last known location of the Dark Lord, Miss Malfoy. I trust that a sharp-minded young woman like yourself understands what that implies."

He was suggesting that Bertha Jorkins had been captured by Voldemort. Which meant Voldemort had already recovered enough strength to overpower a Ministry witch.

It was a truth she had always been warned of, yet had turned away from because she could not bring herself to believe it—and now Karkaroff was pressing it into her hands as well.

"Miss Malfoy. Do you think I'm speaking nonsense?"

First Moody, and now Karkaroff.

The world seemed to have contracted some disease that would kill it unless it kept forcing the truth upon Carina.

"The Dark Lord has recovered all of his power. Do you know why, even so, he has not appeared before his old followers? Because he trusts no one!"

He raised his voice.

"Lucius is no exception. After the Dark Lord disappeared, Lucius publicly denied him at every turn. And his daughter—good heavens—going about with Harry Potter, of all people, as though they were the best of friends!"

"...I'd take issue with that characterisation."

Lies and truths swirled together like a current. The only thing Carina could do in this torrent was carve out one or two escape routes.

She placed a hand over her chest, schooled her expression into something cold and imperious, and denied her own feelings.

"It's true that Harry Potter is fond of me, and I'll admit I see a certain talent in him. But surely that doesn't compare to the Dark Lord?"

"...Heh heh. So you'd discard what's no longer useful? You really are a Malfoy, Miss Malfoy."

Fortunately, a man consumed by suspicion cannot properly read another person's emotions. Karkaroff heard Carina's words and accepted them at face value.

Please let Voldemort be the same way... She offered a brief, silent prayer, then concealed her true thoughts and continued.

"And there seems to be a misunderstanding. Our family has always been among his most devoted servants. When he returns, we will be the first to welcome him. So—"

"I should rest easy? Ha. Miss Malfoy, the Dark Lord trusts no one. The very fact that he hasn't appeared before Lucius, even after recovering his strength, is proof of that!"

"Even so, we have our uses."

Carina said it and looked down at Karkaroff. Unlike him—a man who had survived by betraying and selling out countless comrades—the Malfoys, who had survived through political acumen, still had room to manoeuvre.

Karkaroff's face hardened. The emotions that crossed it were despair and fear.

"M-Miss Malfoy. L-let me see him. Please."

"How would I manage that? I don't even know where he is."

"I-I do. He'll be at the Ministry."

The Ministry? It was the first Carina had heard of this.

But if she showed too much interest, Karkaroff would try to use that information to hold her in his grip.

She pressed him on with a look that said: go on, then.

"No matter how incompetent the Ministry is, they couldn't fail to find one Bertha Jorkins. If they'd deployed their people properly... it must be that he's pulled some strings. So..."

"So?"

"...If you connect me to him, I'll handle the rest myself. Lucius could bring me to the British Ministry. If you do that for me, I'll make sure to mention your contribution..."

His contribution.

Karkaroff's intentions were so base and transparent that Carina could read them in an instant. She arranged a manufactured smile on her face and gave a small nod.

The rain fell. She had cast an umbrella charm to keep it off, but the wind drove the water sideways, soaking her from the hair down.

Her body was cold. The chill of the rain seemed to seep into her veins.

Her sleeves grew damp. Water crept into her shoes. The hem of her skirt turned cold. Carina made her way back to Hogwarts.

"...I have to tell him."

As she stepped inside, Harry happened to come into view. Without thinking, Carina began to move toward him, his name already forming on her lips.

"Har—"

Fortunately, she caught herself just before the sound escaped her throat.

Now that his return was all but certain, Carina could not be seen approaching Harry.

If she did, jackals like Karkaroff would use it to get their claws into her.

"Hagrid would happily put Blast-Ended Skrewts in the maze, you know. Harry, you'd better be ready to deal with those things!"

"Oh, come on. Those are illegal crossbreeds. Even Hagrid has some sense—he wouldn't actually put them in there... would he?"

Harry blinked, and the others nearby dissolved into snickers. Among them, Ginny Weasley was laughing with her eyes—

Ginny Weasley, was it. That family with their insufferable abundance of children managed to produce a daughter who grated on Carina just as thoroughly as the rest of them.

But this time, she found she had no room for jealousy. Carina, who was about to redraw the line between them, had no right to it.

Just once, though.

Carina slipped on the ring and settled into a chair in an empty classroom. She had something to tell him, but she could not be the one to seek him out—so she sat and waited, with no particular end in sight.

The heavy clouds that had filled the sky gradually retreated, and the moon rose through a gap between them. Carina sat motionless, having skipped dinner entirely.

If Harry put on his ring—if he looked at the Marauder's Map—he would find her sitting alone and come to her. Under his Invisibility Cloak.

If he didn't find her...

'I'll have only wasted a little time.'

Not long after the dinner hour had ended, Carina felt the hollow pull of hunger. She had been wandering from place to place since lessons ended without eating a thing, and now her body was making its demands known.

Undignified.

At a moment like this—when no one knew how he might return—to be hungry. Her stomach had truly no sense of occasion.

She was swallowing against it, waiting in silence, when the atmosphere of the room shifted. Carina saw nothing, but she felt the air around her change.

"Watch out for Karkaroff."
