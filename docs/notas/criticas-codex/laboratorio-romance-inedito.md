## 1. Reescritura: llegar al beso sin saltarse el acercamiento

**Lector 46 · trabajo 45, «Rounds».** Base: `borradores/cap-45-rounds.md:223`–241. Sustituir desde «“I don’t,” said Potter; and he smiled» hasta «and went on with her rounds», ambos incluidos. Entrada anterior conservada: «Don’t imagine I won’t» (221). La salida vuelve al final actual del capítulo. No se incluyen párrafos separados como si fueran una cita continua.

**Prueba:** una vacilación real antes del beso, una respuesta voluntaria de Lyra y una separación que no les sale a la primera. La mano derecha permanece en el bolsillo; Lyra aún no conoce la herida. Una sola aproximación y un solo beso: no cambia el cómputo posterior.

<!-- INICIO M1 -->

“I don’t,” said Potter; and he smiled.

She had missed that particular smile. She would have liked it put on record that she was still extremely angry.

He got down off the sill. There was rather less room between them than there had been when he was sitting in it. He seemed to notice this at the same moment she did. His left hand came up, stopped, and went down again.

“Well?” said Lyra.

“Nothing.”

“You’ve been keeping me here for a remarkable amount of nothing.”

He looked at her mouth. Then, hastily, at her badge.

“I could go.”

She took hold of his dressing gown.

It was tartan, much too short for him, and extremely poor material for making a point with. She had meant to pull him nearer. He had already come nearer; they both stopped, and for one dreadful second she could see him trying not to smile again.

“Don’t,” she said.

He stopped trying. That was worse.

She kissed him before he could think of anything to say about it. His left hand found her shoulder, very carefully, and she had a small, furious satisfaction in feeling him forget whatever he had been going to do next. For a moment she forgot as well.

Then she drew back. Not far enough. She was still holding the dressing gown, and his glasses were crooked, and neither of them appeared to know who was to see to that.

“Your badge,” said Potter, in a voice she had not heard at breakfast.

She let go of him. She put it straight. Then her hair. His right hand had stayed in his pocket throughout, which she thought was very like his cheek.

“That was the last one,” said Lyra.

“This year,” said Potter.

“It’s *September*.”

He put the back of his hand against his mouth. His shoulders had begun to go.

Lyra drew herself up to her full height, on the seventh floor, at one o’clock in the morning, with the moon on her badge.

“Five points from Gryffindor,” she said, and went on with her rounds.

**Longitud:** 243 palabras de base → 347 de propuesta (**+104**).

**Comparación:** el original coloca el beso dentro del recuerdo incierto de qué ocurrió; esta versión permite anticiparlo, detenerse y volver a salir. Lyra toma la iniciativa; Harry no ofrece su herida como argumento ni recibe una reconciliación. La broma de septiembre llega después de un pequeño desorden compartido. Mantiene el uniforme de noche, el lugar, el punto de vista, la hora y los cinco puntos. El incremento de longitud compra acciones, no una nueva sensación repetida.

**Decisión:** adoptaría la prueba. Al aplicarla, comprobar que el gesto final de llevarse la mano a la boca conserve la ambigüedad de la base; no sustituirlo por una descripción de los nudillos. El lector recibe la pista, Lyra no la interpreta hasta «Lines».

## 2. Reescritura: pedir que se quede dentro del mismo abrazo

**Lector 50 · trabajo 49, «In Fives».** Base: `borradores/cap-49-in-fives.md:133`–143. Sustituir desde «“Never own a Malfoy,” said Potter» hasta «He did not sound as though he could feel his feet». Antes quedan «Mine», su exposición involuntaria, la petición de repetirlo y la excusa de la venda. Después se mantiene el separador y el análisis de Hermione que empieza en 147.

**Prueba:** desarrollar el abrazo que el original resume en 137–139. No se añade un encuentro ni un segundo beso computable. No se estrena declaración de Lyra. La alegría de ser reclamado no demuestra que reclamar sea seguro: esa distinción debe sobrevivir al resto del capítulo y a «Nobody».

<!-- INICIO M2 -->

“Never own a Malfoy,” said Potter. It was hardly a voice at all. “You said.”

“I said that *you* couldn’t. I said nothing whatever about the other way round.”

He took one step. She had him by the jumper before he had finished it, and then his arms were round her, and for once there was nobody standing far enough away to look superior.

It was a very poor arrangement for keeping one’s dignity. His glasses caught a pin. She had to stop kissing him to free it, with one hand still in his jumper, and he stood quite still while she did so, looking at her as if the pin were the last thing holding up the castle.

“There,” said Lyra.

“Thanks.”

She had not meant to laugh. It came out very small, almost against his mouth. He shut his eyes, and she went back to where she had been.

Afterwards she found that she had her forehead against his collarbone. His heart was going like a rabbit’s. She could feel his hands in the back of her robes, in two fists, and for some time there was nothing to be said about any of it.

She shifted her shoulder. The fists opened at once.

“No,” she said, much too quickly.

He had started to draw back. He stopped.

“There’s a seam,” said Lyra. “It was in my ear. I was moving a seam, Potter, not leaving the country.”

“Right.”

He had gone red. She could see that without lifting her head; it had reached his collar. His hands rested against her back now, quite flat, waiting for something.

“Could you—” He stopped. She waited, with a great deal less composure than she would have preferred. “Stay a bit.”

There was nothing clever in it. That was the difficulty.

She took a fresh handful of the jumper, a little further round, where there was no seam.

“I haven’t gone anywhere.”

“I know.”

He let out a breath. She felt it move the hair above her forehead. They stood there while somebody in a portrait further down the corridor turned over in his sleep, and she had time to think of several things she ought to do, and did none of them.

It was she who let go. It took her some time.

“That was the last one,” said Lyra.

“Eighty-five,” said Potter. He did not sound as though he could feel his feet.

**Longitud:** 162 palabras de base → 404 de propuesta (**+242**).

**Comparación:** la continuidad del beso se interrumpe sólo para liberar una horquilla y reanudarse; sigue siendo el mismo beso y la misma entrada en la cuenta, no dos citas que la gráfica de Hermione deba contar por separado. La muestra conserva el corazón, el contacto con la ropa y que Lyra termine separándose. Cambia la duración resumida por tres estados: torpeza, descanso y una petición.

La apertura de las manos es una acción local de Harry, no una garantía de buen comportamiento para todo el arco. Lyra puede moverse y puede irse; luego el texto mostrará cuándo deja de poder hacerlo. No añadir una frase del narrador que lo declare incapaz de asustarla: sería falsa dentro de este mismo tramo.

**Decisión:** adoptaría la prueba, con una reserva de ritmo: si se vuelve a alargar también el cumplido de «Trophies», hay que escoger entre ambas expansiones. «Stay a bit» da una ganancia distinta de otro piropo.

## 3. Reescritura: escuchar una alegría de la que no se sabe nada

**Lector 76 · trabajo 70, «Cousins».** Base: `borradores/cap-70-cousins.md:93`–97. Sustituir desde «She ate it» hasta «There isn’t one thing wrong», ambos incluidos. Antes quedan el pastel «TAU» y la risa de 91. Después se conserva el separador y la lección de Snape de 101. La interpretación concreta del problema del examen es nueva prosa propuesta: la base sólo da una undécima pregunta difícil, diagramas y una línea final.

**Prueba:** convertir una parte de la tarde contada en conversación. Harry sigue sin saber Arithmancy; su interés tiene una forma comprobable. El final mantiene la equivocación emocional más amplia de Lyra: no se transforma el pastel en demostración de que todo está bien.

<!-- INICIO M3 -->

She ate it. There was salt in it, and a certain amount of shell. It had the texture of a wet Sunday in Surrey.

“Well?” said Potter.

She held out her plate for another piece.

He looked at the plate. Then he looked at her, and something happened to his face that made it necessary to discuss Arithmancy at once.

“The eleventh question was quite disgraceful,” said Lyra. “They gave you two sets of values, and the obvious thing was to treat them separately. Which is what they wanted you to do. It took nearly a whole sheet to discover that it wouldn’t— Give me your notes.”

He gave her his Potions. She turned them over and began at the top, very quickly. By the third line he had leant forward with his fork still in his hand.

“That’s the same thing,” he said, pointing.

“It is not remotely the same thing.”

“It’s the same mark.”

“Oh. Yes. But you’ve got to take account of where it is.” She drew a line between the two. “You see?”

He looked at it with painful attention.

“No.”

She put down the quill.

“Well. Nobody said you had to. You haven’t done the first four years.”

“I didn’t mean stop.”

He had said it rather quickly. He put the fork down, with a piece of cake still on it, and turned the sheet round towards her again.

“Where did you work out they were trying to make you do it wrong?”

“They weren’t trying to—” She looked at the two columns. “There. That’s where it stopped being two questions. Everybody was still doing two questions.”

“But you weren’t.”

“Obviously.”

She had gone a little warm. It was absurd; he had understood practically nothing. She took the quill back, and started further up the page, so that he could see where the trouble had begun.

He asked one more question. It was a very stupid question, but it was about the right part, and by the time she had finished answering it she had forgotten to be embarrassed. She sat cross-legged on the bald rug, with the window open and the swifts going over, and told him how she had got to the last line.

There was no room left on the paper. She drew it in icing on the edge of the plate.

He looked down at it, and up.

“That’s *you*, that is,” said Harry. “All of that.”

She kissed him. It was only a small one; he had crumbs on his chin, and she had to lean across the plate to do it. The sun lay along the side of his glasses, and she had a pain in her chest from laughing; and she thought, as clearly as she’d ever thought anything: *I got all of it. There isn’t one thing wrong.*

<!-- FIN M3 -->

**Longitud:** 207 palabras de base → 471 de propuesta (**+264**).

**Comparación:** el original ya ofrece una imagen buena, la línea dibujada en glaseado. La muestra la conserva como consecuencia de haberse quedado sin papel, y conserva la atención como algo que Harry elige hacer. Él no valida el resultado matemático: reconoce el momento en que ella vio una relación que los demás no veían. Lyra empieza acelerada, se protege cuando no la entiende, descubre que no la está despidiendo y retoma su propia alegría. Esa secuencia es la ganancia.

No se altera la nota del examen ni se afirma una calificación oficial antes de recibirla. «I got it all» sigue siendo su percepción. No se ocupa tiempo de Ron o Hermione, no cambia el pastel ni su autoría, y el siguiente corte sigue siendo hacia Snape y el peligro. Si el contenido académico añadido no encaja con el sistema de Arithmancy que Edén prefiera, cambiar los valores y las marcas por el proceso concreto elegido; mantener la pregunta de Harry y la petición de continuar, que son el mecanismo de la prueba.

**Decisión:** es la primera intervención que adoptaría. Da una alegría nueva a la pareja sin recurrir a otra emergencia ni competir con «Yours».

## 4. Escena nueva N1: querer que no termine una hora normal

**Lector 63 · trabajo 59, «In Public».** Insertar entre `borradores/cap-59-in-public.md:121` y `:123`, después de «nobody gave it a mark» y antes del separador que da paso a «Theodore Nott lent her a book…» en 125. Es el mismo viernes del repaso. No modifica «Nobody said anything for the rest of the hour»: empieza una vez acabada. La prosa no introduce el reloj que la habitación sólo tendrá en «Ask Me».

**Prueba:** pedir compañía sin convertirla en otro ejercicio ni en otra curación. Se conserva la obligación posterior de las rondas. No hay beso ni deducción de puntos.

<!-- INICIO M4 -->

Potter capped the ink and began to put the goblins away.

He did it with unusual thoroughness. He squared the parchment, found the lid of the bottle where it had rolled under the chair, and gathered up three quills, of which only one was his.

“Must you make such a business of it?” said Lyra.

He looked at the quills.

“You said we’d finished.”

“We have.”

“Right.”

He put them in his bag. She watched him do the buckle. He got it wrong, took it out and tried again. It was an extremely slow buckle, and she could have had him through the door in half the time, if that had been what she wanted.

“You needn’t go at once.”

The buckle stopped.

“Have I got some more?”

“No, Potter. There are no more goblins. The goblins have gone to bed. You may take your hand out of the bag.”

He did. She had her book open across her knees. She had been on the same sentence for some time.

“Five minutes,” she said.

He looked at her. Then at the timetable over the fire.

“Before your rounds?”

“I am aware of my rounds.”

He sat down on the rug again, with his back against the chair. He had left the bag shut. After a moment he looked up.

“What are we doing?”

She laid one hand, lightly, on the top of his head, and turned it back towards the fire.

“This.”

“Oh.”

He stayed facing the right way. His shoulder had come to rest against her knee. She moved the book so that its corner wasn’t sticking into his neck, and for a while there was only the fire.

“You can stop looking pleased,” said Lyra.

“You can’t see my face.”

She could hear it perfectly well. She left her hand where it was until she really did have to collect the book and get up.

**Comparación y coste:** antes no hay escena que sustituir: ésta añade su extensión completa. El estudio previo y la siguiente secuencia de Nott siguen siendo escenas distintas. El breve movimiento de Harry hacia la salida convierte el paso del tiempo en una resistencia concreta. La mano en el pelo ya tiene precedente en «Scraps»; no se estrena confianza ni costumbre de nombres. Ella pide; él entiende tarde; los dos pueden estar callados después de haber elegido hacerlo.

Financiación propuesta: acortar las recapitulaciones del gráfico y de los ensayos públicos en el propio 59, como explica N1 en el informe. No recortar la tarde de Hermione, la correspondencia de Ron ni la partida de cartas de los demás. Si no se desea aumentar el capítulo, puede reservarse esta muestra y adoptar únicamente las tres sustituciones. Es una opción completa y aplicable, no una escena que el manuscrito necesite para ser comprensible.

**Decisión:** adoptaría después de las tres reescrituras. No sumarla a otra escena nueva de pedir un minuto en «Furniture».

## 5. Escena nueva N2: Ron y Hermione encuentran un paso

**Lector 65 · trabajo 60b, «Three».** Insertar entre `borradores/cap-60b-three.md:87`, después de que Harry y Ron bajen de la mesa, y `:89`, «It was Seamus who heard it». La radio ya está puesta y ya se ha bailado en 79. Se conserva el punto de vista de Harry. El baile no inaugura besos, declaración ni noviazgo: Ron aún podrá preguntarse qué están haciendo en `borradores/cap-65b-bothy.md:97`.

**Prueba:** el grupo tiene una vida amorosa que no se limita a aprobar o molestar a Harry y Lyra. La escena no añade una aparición de Lyra a una fiesta que pertenece a Ron.

<!-- INICIO M5 -->

Hermione was waiting at the bottom. She had got a streamer wound round one wrist, and she was trying to look as though she had only happened to stop there.

“Will you?” she said to Ron.

He looked up at the ceiling.

“Do it again? I don’t think that table’s going to—”

“Dance.”

“Oh.”

He looked at her hand. She had held it out, quite steadily, in the middle of the common room. Harry watched the discovery go up from Ron’s collar. It seemed to take him rather longer than the third save.

“Yeah,” said Ron. “If you— Yeah.”

He took it. They went about two feet, and stopped. Hermione said something that Harry couldn’t hear. Ron looked down, and moved his foot.

“Sorry.”

“You haven’t trodden on me.”

“I was going to.”

“You don’t have to apologise in advance.”

She had gone pink. Ron put his other hand at her back, with the attention he had given his new broom on the day it came. For a moment neither of them moved at all. Then she counted something under her breath, and he nodded, and they went the same way.

Harry found his mug. It had got behind the plant.

“That’s not it,” said Ron, after a while. “I mean, I don’t think that’s what they’re playing.”

“I know.”

“Oh. Right.”

He stopped trying to match the trumpets. They went round quite slowly, with an elbow out, and Hermione’s hand on it. She seemed to have forgotten about putting it anywhere else.

Ron said something. She laughed, looking up at him, and he missed the next step altogether. This time she stood on his foot. They both looked down, and neither of them apologised.

The music went quiet. Harry took a drink, and found that it wasn’t his mug.

Seamus had his hand on the dial.

**Longitud:** escena nueva de **307 palabras**; incremento bruto de 307.

**Comparación y coste:** adición completa, financiable acortando parte del inventario de imitaciones y bailes de 55 y 79. Se mantienen a Neville, Ginny y la planta, el anuncio de los Cannons, los dos Sickles y la rodilla final. La frase «It was Seamus who heard it» vuelve a encajar inmediatamente después: el dial prepara la noticia, no la sustituye.

La vergüenza aquí es de otra clase: Hermione se atreve a pedir algo sin un argumento y Ron intenta hacerlo bien en público. Ninguno recibe la voz de Lyra ni necesita una respuesta ingeniosa. Harry puede observarlos sin la vieja cuenta de quién llega primero; la fiesta ya le permite participar de una alegría del grupo. Es un momento, no una resolución estable de su soledad. El placer de ellos no se presenta como tratamiento de Harry.

**Decisión:** opcional. Es una experiencia distinta y cabe en la fiesta, pero si el capítulo crece demasiado mantendría su versión actual. No lo aplicaría a costa del entrenamiento de Ron, de su orgullo por las tres paradas o del efecto del final sobre la Occlumency.

## 6. Variante más intensa: quedarse contra el abrigo en la nieve

**Lector 57 · trabajo 53, «The Button Warmer».** Base: `borradores/cap-53-the-button-warmer.md:201`–215. Sustituir desde la risa que sale como nube hasta su marcha tras las amigas. Entrada anterior: «Well. I hope somebody made a great deal of money on it» (199). Salida siguiente conservada: el perro de Black en 217. No se mezclan aquí las frases de la nota de Regulus con las del contacto: esa conversación anterior queda intacta.

**Prueba de límite:** prolongar una iniciativa que el original mantiene mínima. Harry conserva las manos en los bolsillos; no se adelanta el momento de volver a tocarlos que pertenece a la colina. La escena reconoce que Lyra ha tomado la decisión de acercarse. No borra lo del brazo ni convierte el regalo para Narcissa en pago por el contacto.

<!-- INICIO M6 -->

He laughed. It came out of him in a surprised white cloud in the cold air, the real one; and he stopped it almost at once, and looked at her over it. His hands stayed in his pockets.

She could have gone then. Crabbe and Goyle were a long way up the lane, and nobody was going to ask her why she had spent so long discussing a button.

She took one step, and put her forehead against the front of his robes.

He stopped breathing.

“You may go on with that,” said Lyra, to the wool.

He let it out very carefully. She felt it come back in. He smelled of wet wool and Weasley’s crumpets, and there was something extremely stupid about standing in the snow with one’s gloves in one’s hand when one had been brought up to know better.

She did not move.

“Malfoy.”

“What?”

“I don’t know what to do.”

It had hardly any voice in it. She shut her eyes. The folded paper for her mother was against her ribs, and the brass button was somewhere between them, warm through his collar.

“Nothing. You might try it. It would be quite a novelty.”

He made a small sound that might have been a laugh if he had had any more air for it. His hands did not come out. He moved one foot instead, to stand a little more steadily, and that was all.

She had not expected that to make it harder to leave.

“Another moment,” said Lyra.

“Yeah.”

The snow settled on his shoulders. She watched one flake disappear into the dark wool beside her hand, and then another. At last she stood up straight, and put on her gloves, one finger at a time.

“That didn’t count.”

“No,” said Potter. There was hardly any sound in it.

She turned and went up the lane after the others.

**Longitud:** 189 palabras de base → 316 de propuesta (**+127**).

**Qué cambia:** la suspensión que el original deja casi insoportable se convierte en una estancia que Lyra solicita y consigue. La respiración tiene una tarea emocional, no sensual: Harry no sabe qué puede hacer y ella le pide que no haga nada. Mantiene el frío, la carta que no le pertenece abrir, el botón y la iniciativa de Lyra. No introduce un abrazo recíproco ni el uso íntimo de los nombres.

**Por qué no la adoptaría:** el original logra más con menos. En 207, «Nothing about him moved, not an inch» permite sentir todo lo que Harry se impide hacer sin darle todavía una rutina nueva que les funcione. La versión intensa verbaliza y vuelve habitable ese instante. Les regala un pequeño refugio a dos capítulos del acontecimiento que hará necesaria la colina; disminuye el contraste entre el primer contacto mínimo después del miedo y la negociación mucho más ardua de la muñeca. También se acerca demasiado al mecanismo de N1, pedir un rato, cuando aquí importa poder iniciar algo y retirarlo sin que él lo cobre.

La probaría en voz alta para comparar, pero conservaría la base. Si falta calor en ese sector, lo buscaría después de los términos de la colina, no antes. El coste del regalo y la negativa de Vanessa a volver a confiar deben seguir presentes aunque Harry y Lyra quieran acercarse.
