Severus Snape had finally obtained the Defence Against the Dark Arts position he had long coveted. The resulting vacancy in Potions was filled by an old professor named Horace Slughorn—appointed personally by Dumbledore.

Carina's first impression of him was that he moved with the unhurried ease of an aged bear, and was every bit as cunning.

From the moment he stepped into the classroom, Slughorn had greeted Harry Potter and Blaise Zabini with conspicuous warmth. The reason was obvious enough. Those two were either the most famous students in the room, or the ones with the most powerful connections.

The reason Carina was not among them was equally simple.

It was Slughorn's way of making clear that he had no particular interest in cultivating ties with the sons and daughters of Death Eaters.

As if to mirror her mood, four cauldrons bubbled and churned at the front of the room. Carina kept her expression neutral and took her seat among the Slytherins. The underground classroom was thick with the strange, mingling scents of the potions.

The smell was something like the familiar warmth of a broom cupboard, or the clean fragrance of a freshly laundered cloak. It was a pleasant, heady scent—sweet as fine wine—yet something about it made Carina uneasy, and she pressed her sleeve against her nose.

Through the drifting haze, Slughorn spoke.

"Now, I've brewed a few of these simply because I thought they'd be of interest. Every one of them is something you'll be expected to produce if you continue on to N.E.W.T. level."

He gestured toward the cauldron sitting directly in front of the Slytherin table. Carina leaned forward to examine the liquid. It looked like plain water.

"Does anyone know what this potion is?"

Colourless and odourless. She knew of one potion that fit the description. But before the name had fully formed in her mind, Hermione—who had always been first to these moments—already had her hand in the air.

"Veritaserum. This colourless, odourless potion forces the drinker to tell the truth with as little as three drops."

"Excellent!"

Slughorn beamed and moved on to the cauldron before the Ravenclaw table, where a mud-like potion simmered slowly.

"This one is rather well known—it's been mentioned in Ministry pamphlets on more than one occasion… Does anyone know what it is?"

"Polyjuice Potion! It transforms you into someone else!"

"Splendid, splendid! And this one?"

Hermione's hand shot up again, and Slughorn's expression shifted to something faintly pained.

"Amortentia! The most powerful love potion in existence!"

"Correct. I begin to feel rather foolish asking. You'd have recognised it by the distinctive mother-of-pearl sheen, I imagine?"

"And by the steam—it rises in a spiral, which is quite unusual!"

Hermione was practically glowing as she continued.

"This potion is known to smell differently to each person, according to what attracts them. For me, right now, it smells like—"

It was a performance. The moment Hermione Granger was added to Slughorn's list of promising students was almost physically irritating—made worse by the fact that Carina herself was not on it.

It seemed she would not be getting on with this old professor. Carina had nearly resigned herself to that conclusion when—

"Shall we get on with the lesson, then?"

"Professor, you haven't told us about that cauldron yet."

"Ho!"
