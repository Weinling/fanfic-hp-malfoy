Carina and Harry bought a spacious house with a garden. It was a beautiful building—ivy climbing the low stone walls, the exterior painted a clean, bright white. Having once tasted the comfort that money could buy, Carina had no intention of returning to anything less, and the steady stream of funds flowing into their account was more than enough to ensure she never had to.

The two of them filled the house with things. Furniture ordered from department stores arrived by delivery; the tedious work of setting it all up was dispatched with a few well-placed spells. And so, piece by piece, they built themselves a warm and comfortable home.

The late morning sun pressed through the curtains and announced the day to Carina. She opened her eyes slowly. The first thing to greet her was the high white ceiling. The second was the soft, even sound of Harry breathing beside her.

Carina turned her head without thinking. Harry lay asleep, his glasses set aside on the nightstand.

Perhaps it was all the times he had walked the edge of death—Harry had always seemed far older than his years. But asleep like this, unguarded and still, he looked exactly his age. Just a boy, like any other.

Carina smiled faintly. She tucked her hair behind her ear and leaned toward him.

One of the unwritten rules of Potter House: whoever woke first was responsible for waking the other.

She pressed her lips to his to do exactly that—and Harry opened his eyes with a mischievous grin. Before she could react, he pulled her into his arms and rolled, taking her with him.

Rules, of course, always attract those who would exploit them. Harry had taken to lying awake in the mornings, feigning sleep. Being the one to kiss first was its own pleasure—but watching her wake, watching her push her hair back and lean in close, was a happiness all its own.

"That's—cheating," Carina mumbled.

Her protest dissolved somewhere beneath the duvet.

They made their way to the bathroom together. They sat in the tub with their arms around each other, cataloguing the marks they'd left on each other through the night. With the hot water running, Harry worked his hands slowly over Carina's body, and she let herself go soft against him, a low sound escaping her lips.

She shivered slightly and looked at him with a plaintive expression. Harry shook his head, firm, and continued the massage—nothing more.

There had been an incident, not long ago, when they'd gotten carried away in the shower and Carina had spent the next day in bed with a fever. Since then, Harry had held to a strict policy: hands only in the bathroom, and absolutely nothing further.

It only delayed things by a few minutes, of course.

By the time they sat down to breakfast, they collected on everything they'd held back—with interest. Each was simultaneously debtor and creditor, and both were ruthless about collecting.

What they tasted most was each other's lips. After that, the curve of a neck. If you counted a few more things—the salt of skin, the brush of hair—then at last, somewhat begrudgingly, came the rye bread Harry had baked with a spell.

When hunger finally drove one of them to take a bite, the other would reach for it with some primal, irrational jealousy. Whether it was the lips that envied the bread, or the bread that had stolen the lips, was impossible to say. They simply snatched and counter-snatched until their mouths found each other instead.

Which was why their lips remained, by a wide margin, the thing they tasted most.

When the mood ran hot enough, Harry would lift Carina and carry her back to the bedroom. When they were a little tired, or the atmosphere settled into something softer and less urgent, they would finally get dressed and turn to something more productive.

Carina would open her books and work through the N.E.W.T. curriculum. Her pride would not permit her to leave Hogwarts without completing the advanced coursework—even if she was completing it from a house in the Muggle world, on her own schedule. Independent study had its difficulties, but Harry helped her through them, one concept at a time.
