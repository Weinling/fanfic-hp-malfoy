"Harry. I have good news and bad news. Which would you like to hear first?"

"You're going to tell me both either way, aren't you? What does the order matter? Just get on with it."

"Hmm. Then I shall begin with the good news... We have found a new Horcrux."

Dumbledore gestured to the ring resting on the table. A silver ring set with a large stone, it had been reduced to ruin by the Sword of Gryffindor.

"I have told you something of Tom Riddle's origins before, Harry. His mother, Merope Gaunt, was a witch born into the Gaunt family—a pureblood line of wretched poverty, yet direct descendants of Salazar Slytherin. And his father..."

"Tom Riddle the First. The Muggle Voldemort despised so utterly."

"Yes. He was a direct heir of Slytherin, and yet a half-blood. His mother's family was pureblood but degraded and destitute; his father's family wealthy and comfortable, but Muggle. He was born into that contradiction. Raised in an orphanage from infancy, he never knew the faces of his parents—yet he carried within him a vague, consuming mixture of longing and hatred for the very idea of family."

The ring, which must once have been beautiful, had lost all its light and become nothing more than wreckage. Dumbledore murmured with something like sorrow.

"And so Gaunt's Ring—this precious heirloom passed down through generations—was worthy of becoming a Horcrux. And a Horcrux it was. After tracing Tom Riddle's past, I found this ring in the home of his mother's family."

The good news Dumbledore had brought was that another Horcrux had been destroyed. Even under the Ministry's constant interference, he had followed the trail and found it—and unmade it.

But what the existence of this Horcrux implied...

"...Is this ring an artefact of one of Hogwarts' founders?"

Harry asked the question flatly, already half-certain of the bad news to come. Dumbledore shook his head with a look of quiet regret.

"No. Salazar Slytherin's artefact is that locket—and that alone. There may be others, but none were found among the Gaunts. This ring is an heirloom their ancestors preserved. It predates Hogwarts itself..."

Dumbledore stopped mid-sentence when he saw Harry's expression. Harry's face had gone still, and beneath the stillness lay a deep, unmistakable exhaustion.

After a long silence, Harry finally spoke.

"The Horcruxes... there could be far more than we thought? It isn't only artefacts of Hogwarts' founders that can become Horcruxes..."

Dumbledore nodded. Harry pressed his hands together and exhaled slowly. The tendons stood white against his knuckles.

Dumbledore spoke in a tone meant to comfort.

"I am currently seeking a friend who may know more about the Horcruxes. He was a professor at Hogwarts during Tom Riddle's time as a student. With his help, we may be able to determine how many Horcruxes there are."

"When?"

Harry's voice was bleak.

"We haven't even found Ravenclaw's artefact yet, and now there could be several more Horcruxes of unknown identity? What then? Are we meant to search for things we don't even know exist? Like Luna hunting for Crumple-Horned Snorkacks?"

"What a curious name. What is that?"

"That's not the point!"

Harry snapped, shoving himself to his feet. His face was raw with fury and something darker beneath it.

"Professor Dumbledore. I'm withdrawing from school."

"I cannot permit that."

"I'll strap Umbridge to a broomstick and send her to the moon on my way out. There's nothing left to learn in this place except how much I despise people."

"You know as well as I do that you cannot do that—any more than I can."

"How much longer do I have to stay here? There are an obscene number of Horcruxes. The bloody Ministry obstructs everything at every turn. That woman Umbridge looks at me like she'd like to have me for dinner. And I just keep enduring it. Enduring, and enduring, and enduring. Why? They answer to no one and do as they please, while I'm supposed to sit here and take it? Suffer through a school where I can barely learn anything? Why should I—"

Harry's voice cracked into something raw. Dumbledore watched him without moving.

"You said the fighting was for adults, didn't you? But that's not how it is! At least, that's not how they see it! Death Eaters send their own children into Dumbledore's hands while conspiring on every front imaginable! Umbridge isn't just after me anymore—she's keeping Luna in detention now too! Simply because her father edits The Quibbler!"

"Harry."
