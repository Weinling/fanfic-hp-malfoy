She pointed at his forehead.

He put one hand over the wrong eyebrow.

She lost what remained of her breath. He saw that, and threw his head back, laughing without a sound, with the rain going into his mouth; and she put the Firebolt’s nose down before he could recover.

It was only a little drop. She let the gale carry her sideways, and cut back under him. He had gone the other way. For one glorious instant he was looking for her in an entirely empty piece of sky.

Then he found her. She saw his mouth open in outrage. She lifted her chin at him.

He rolled, came down from above and went past her in a scarlet blur. She heard nothing. She could see him whooping. She kicked at the tail of his broom, just caught it, and he looked back at her with his whole face, furious and delighted, and she was going after him before she had thought of it.

It was perhaps half a minute. It was perhaps not even that.

Then he looked down. She saw his face change, and she saw it too.

Hermione had already moved. She stopped.

“The lamp,” said Ron.

“Oh. Yes.”

She held it lower. He had enormous fingers, and there was a cold sort of numbness in them. He got the ends crossed, lost one, and started again. He could feel her standing there. It was the most ordinary thing in the world, and he had to concentrate very hard on the lace.

He got it done. He pulled both loops, much too hard, and stood up.

She raised the lamp. He had the chart safe; he put a hand over the pocket to be sure. There was a new column in it, and a great deal that hadn’t gone away.

“Fifth floor,” said Hermione.

“Yeah.”

He went up the first stair. She came after him, with the light where they could both see, and he waited at the turn before going on.

## D. Variante límite: un beso más largo en la escalera

**Lector 91 · trabajo 85, «It’s a Nice One».** Sustituir `borradores/cap-85-its-a-nice-one.md:73`–79, desde «She saw it reach him» hasta «to dry». Entrada anterior conservada: «Bacon» y la aclaración de que se lo ha dicho a él, 69–71. Salida siguiente: separador de 81 y domingo de 83.

**Objetivo de la prueba:** dar más espacio a la aproximación y a la separación sin añadir diálogo oído por la marca ni permitir que Harry la sujete. Lyra inicia el contacto; él mantiene las manos en los bolsillos. No se presupone permiso para hacer otra cosa. Es una intensificación del mismo encuentro, no una escena nueva que deba encajarse en otra tarde.

She saw it reach him. His chest had stopped. He stood two steps above her with his hands in his pockets, and his eyes behind the mended glasses went very wide and filled up, quite slowly, from the bottom. His lips parted.

*Don’t*, she thought. *Don’t speak. Not one word.*

He shut his mouth. He pushed his hands down so far that she saw his shoulders move.

She went up one step. He didn’t come down. She had time to look at the cold stone between their boots, and to think that she could go on past him. She went up the other.

There was a thread of wool caught on his collar. She took hold of the edge beside it, between one grey woollen finger and thumb, and pulled him down a very little. His glasses were crooked. She had meant to do something about those first, and then she found that she had stood on her toes and kissed him instead.

His mouth was chapped and cold, and it was shaking. She felt him draw back by the smallest amount, as if he had been startled; then he was there again, with his hands still where she could not see them.

She could have stopped. She found she was holding the collar rather hard. She loosened her fingers, and for a moment they stood like that, very close, not speaking, with the snow melting from her cloak on to the stair.

He had shut his eyes. That was almost more than she could bear.

She let go. She smoothed the collar down once, over the thread, and went past him. She did not look back. At the turn she noticed that her left hand was pressed against her ribs, and that she was smiling, very stiffly, at a wall.

**Qué se pierde:** la prueba aumenta el coste emocional de cada segundo y hace del encuentro una pequeña estancia. El frasco no compra este beso y el beso no remedia «Please come». Prefiero gastar las palabras nuevas en B. Se conserva esta prueba para que la decisión pueda comprobarse comparando dos escenas completas, no sólo dos frases.
