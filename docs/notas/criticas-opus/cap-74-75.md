# Crítica del 74 y el 75

**74: 9/10. 75: 8,5/10.** Lo mejor del tramo.

**La cocina** se gana la felicidad. **La cena** da miedo, pero mide 1.612 palabras y cuatro revelaciones; el Bishop la salva. **El armario** le pega: aquí no puede romperse delante de nadie, y las esquinas rematan.

**«It was *mine*»** es creíble porque es verdad y porque él ya lo quería; 75:109 lo explica antes de tiempo. **El libro largo** no es acta: «who does not open things» lo justifica. **Bones:** la jugada es buena (forzar la elección), pero cojea en Crouch (corrección 1). **Draco** duele; es el mejor final desde el 39.

**Riddle:** una sola voz, y varía en el 75 al reír y callar. Riesgo: «so + adjetivo», seis en el 74.

**Cuentas correctas:** 24 (12 × 2), 21 galletas, 6 de junio jueves, 19 nov. (28:141), 26 viernes, 31 miércoles, Crucio, Bode.

**Tics.** «There», 19 y 17 (3,9 y 3,7 por mil). «It», 43 y 47 (8,8 y 10,3). «As long as it takes»: 3.

## Incoherencias y correcciones

1. **Grave. 75:147.** Crouch dirigía Cooperación Internacional (35:191, 40:65), y una Bones lista habría cambiado las guardas.
There had been a time… done as he was told. Barty Crouch’s son had brought away a list, and the keys, and the words for the wards. → Two Heads of Department held the keys to all four, and for a year and a half one of them had done as he was told. Madam Bones had had the wards reset the week he was found. They could only be reset from the old words, and Barty Crouch’s son had brought away a list of those.

2. **Grave. 75:139.** Domingo 14 (75:5) + 19 = 2 de agosto.
It took her nineteen days. → It took her twelve days.

3. **75:43 (extra-1:63, 55:67; choca con 75:53).** Es de papel de la casa.
a thin green account book, of the kind sold for a Sickle in Diagon Alley, rather rubbed → a small flat book in dark green boards, with a loop for a pencil, rather rubbed

4. **74:125 (58:183).** Eso lo dijo; lo escrito es otra cosa.
“*He has nits*,” he read, “*and he’s afraid that people will find out.*” He looked up. “I preferred the first version, I think. → “*He has nits*,” he read, “*and it preys upon him.*” He looked up. “I preferred it the way he said it, I think.

5. **74:19 contra 74:51.**
He considered her for a moment. “No,” said Draco. → He went on looking at the wall. “No,” said Draco.

6. **75:141 (62:43).** with nothing on the outside, in a plain round careful hand → with nothing on the outside and four lines within, in a plain round careful hand

7. **75:169 (74:15).** since she came home → since the first night

8. **74:111 (72:67 y 72:155).** and eleven of my friends behind him → and my friends in a line behind him

9. **75:109.** Quitar: She didn’t have to act it. That was what she’d understood, on the nursery stool.

10. **74:153 («No X»).** It wasn’t crying. She had done crying. She had done that in an Owlery, with her nose running, and she knew how it went. → She had cried in an Owlery, with her nose running, and she knew how that went.

11. **74:109.** Quitar las cursivas: el narrador se cita a sí mismo.

12. **74:141.** so attentive → most attentive

13. **75:177.** Dos réplicas seguidas de Draco. Antes: He moved one of them, a very little, in its paper.

14. **75:199 (cara que se cierra).** his face was shut and clean → his face was clean

15. **Plazos.** 74:171: for about as long as it takes to read a line → once. 75:129: for perhaps as long as it takes to breathe in, and out → for a breath or two
