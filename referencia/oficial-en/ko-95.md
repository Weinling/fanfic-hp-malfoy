# Traducción inglesa oficial — coreano 95: Easter Vacation

Arriving at King's Cross Station for the Easter holiday, Harry waved goodbye to his friends. Any other Easter, they would all have headed to the Burrow together—but today he had somewhere else to be. They agreed to catch up by owl post, and Harry parted from them, heading in the opposite direction.

There, a family stood waiting—the sort that belonged in a portrait hung in a castle gallery.

Lucius Malfoy was watching Harry with the expression of a man chewing something deeply unpleasant. Narcissa Malfoy, by contrast, wore a rather cordial look. Carina kept her face carefully neutral, though her eyes moved anxiously between Harry and Lucius. Draco stood quietly tucked against Narcissa's side—nothing like the troublemaker Harry knew him to be.

Harry strode toward them.

"Good afternoon, Mr. Malfoy."

"…Yes. Potter. Charmed."

The moment the words were out, both of them locked eyes in a silent contest of wills. Harry and Lucius squared off in a wordless standoff. Carina pressed her fingers to her temple with a pained look but did not intervene.

Just then, a tall wizard with dark skin approached Lucius, removing his hat and extending a hand.

"Good afternoon, Mr. Malfoy. Kingsley Shacklebolt—I've been assigned to protect Harry Potter."

"Ah, Mr. Shacklebolt. I've heard the name often enough."

Lucius replied at a leisurely pace.

"Protecting Harry Potter, you say? What a shame. If he requires protection, he ought to be kept somewhere safe—somewhere familiar, not a stranger's home. Somewhere like, say, our manor…"

"Very kind of you, but please don't trouble yourself. I'll be watching from a distance and come running the moment Harry calls. Enjoy your holiday, Harry."

Kingsley gave Harry a single nod and Disapparated. Lucius turned his gaze back to Harry with the slow deliberateness of a man swallowing something foul. Harry smiled pleasantly and gestured ahead.

"Well then, shall we be off?"

"…What a pity, Potter. I had a car brought for my family."

Lucius curled his lip. The expression bore an uncomfortable resemblance to Snape's most disagreeable look, and Harry felt his mood sour slightly even with Carina standing right there.

"Regrettably, it seats four. I'm afraid there's no room for you. Though if the boot would do, I'd be more than happy to offer that."

"Father!"

Even Carina's sharp rebuke did nothing to shift Lucius's manner. It was a petty move. Harry had been prepared to let things slide, for Carina's sake if nothing else—but Harry Potter was not the sort of person who absorbed provocation quietly.

"Heh."

Harry raised a hand to cover his face as laughter began to shake his shoulders. Lucius's scowl deepened—and then the voice that followed made him go rigid, eyes widening as he stepped back.

A cold light entered Harry's gaze, fixed on Lucius. That arrogant bearing. That glacial tone. The unhurried, reptilian quality of movement that concealed the capacity for sudden, lethal speed. Lucius had encountered this before.

Harry Potter was doing an impression of Voldemort.

"Lucius. My old friend…. You have always professed your loyalty to me, and yet—yes. What you have ever offered me has always been what you yourself had no use for. The shelter you provided, the devotion you pledged—always the spare, never the first…."

Lucius's hand shot inside his robes and seized his wand. His voice came out in a trembling rasp.

"If you're mocking me—"

"Oh-ho. And now you presume to speak to me as an equal…. Had it not been for Abraxas, you would have been dead long ago, and no one would have thought it strange…."

When Harry's mouth produced details from a generation past, Lucius went pale with genuine terror.

He knew, of course, that Voldemort's soul had been sealed inside Harry Potter. He knew it was not supposed to surface until Harry's next birthday. This was clearly a prank—Harry imitating Voldemort for sport.

But fear is not an emotion that yields easily to reason.

"M-my… my Lord…?"

What if the thing looking out through those eyes was Voldemort? What if this was the being capable of destroying his entire family in a single moment?

One chance in ten thousand. He could not dismiss even that. Lucius, gripped by dread, found himself bowing toward Harry before he had consciously decided to do so.

That was when Carina, having endured quite enough, walked over and slapped Harry sharply on the shoulder. Lucius flinched and cried out.

"Carina! What are you doing?!"

But the thing he feared did not happen. Harry yelped in a thoroughly undignified fashion and rubbed his shoulder. Carina, leaving her father's alarm behind her, fixed Harry with a sharp look as he pulled a wounded face.

"Enough, Harry."

"Ah—sorry. Though that was at least partially self-defence, wasn't it—all right. Sorry."

In front of Carina, Harry's expression softened and he raised both hands in surrender. When she still didn't look away, he offered a lopsided smile and dipped his head toward Lucius.

"My apologies. I'll restrain myself, Mr. Malfoy."

"You—you! You're mocking me!"

"Lucius, my friend. Come to think of it, you have never once smiled in my presence…. Tell me—does it feel better, now that I've teased you a little?"

"Eek!"

Carina raised her voice again.

"Harry!"

"All right, sorry. But I'd appreciate it if he'd stop with the pointless resistance. I can only bow my head for so long when he keeps at it like that."

He needled without ever quite saying anything untrue. Harry Potter was a man who preferred a well-aimed barb to outright capitulation. Watching him grin, Carina exhaled deeply and moved to her father's side.

"Father, please stop the posturing. It's a waste of everyone's time."

"…Fine."

Lucius turned without another word and walked toward the car park. Harry fell into step beside them with perfect composure, as though it were the most natural thing in the world. Positioning himself between Carina and Draco, he greeted Narcissa with easy familiarity.

"Good afternoon, Mother-in-law."

"Yes…."
Narcissa accepted the greeting. Lucius could not.

"I never gave you permission to call me Father-in-law!"

"I didn't call you Father-in-law either, did I."

And so it began again. Carina exhaled quietly and nudged Narcissa; Narcissa gave a small nod. Narcissa took hold of Lucius's arm and steered him forward. Carina pinched the back of Harry's hand. In their respective pairs, the group made their way toward the King's Cross car park.

"??"

Only Draco, caught between the two factions, walked on in complete bewilderment.

At last, the Malfoy family arrived at the car park. Harry took one look at Lucius Malfoy's vehicle and smiled with thinly veiled contempt.

"A Rolls-Royce? How interesting—purebloods turn out to be no different from Muggles after all."

"Hmph. If one must use Muggle means to avoid Muggle eyes, one uses the finest available."

"I'd only hope you didn't use a spell to avoid paying for it."

"No need to worry. It was a gift."

"Ha. One can only hope the person who gave it did so of their own free will. Given your usual conduct, I rather doubt it."

"That is enough!"

Carina, at her limit, grabbed Harry by the arm. In the meantime, Narcissa had opened the driver's door, bundled Lucius inside, and made her way to the passenger seat.

Harry let himself be pulled along, still grumbling.

"Honestly. He brought the Rolls-Royce on purpose, didn't he. A Rolls-Royce has four seats, so it seats four. So you can fly here or run here or do whatever you like. That was the plan, wasn't it?"

"You're wrong. Do you really think Father is that petty?"

Before Harry could answer yes, Carina covered his mouth and pointed to the car.

"He put an Undetectable Extension Charm on the inside. There's room for luggage and everything. There'll be a seat for you…."

Carina said this as she opened the rear door. But the interior, contrary to expectation, was a perfectly ordinary Rolls-Royce. Spacious for a Muggle car, certainly—but with only two seats.

Harry tilted his head.

"Hmm. Either Mr. Malfoy is very petty, or this car is very small. One of the two, Carina."

"That's strange. I was certain this car had an Extension Charm on it…."

Carina looked toward Lucius with dawning suspicion.

"Father—did you remove the Extension Charm? To stop Harry from getting in?"

"What are you saying, Carina? Someone might hear you and get the wrong idea."

Lucius gave a small, satisfied smile.

"Unauthorised Extension Charms are illegal, are they not. I, of course, have done nothing illegal—but what if some person with a grudge against us were to 'misunderstand' and report us?"

It was a moment that laid Lucius's pettiness entirely bare. Harry snorted and promptly sat down. Though if pettiness was the game, Harry was no less capable of playing it. Above all, Harry Potter had been notorious since his first year for merciless retaliation.

Having claimed the back seat, Harry reached out, caught Carina by the waist, and pulled her down onto his lap.

The soft weight of her settled against him. Carina startled at the move and squirmed for a moment—then caught the mischief in his expression and let out a slow breath. Harry held her firmly in place and turned a triumphant smile on Lucius.

"Can't be helped. We'll just have to share."

Lucius, who had been sitting in the driver's seat looking very pleased with himself, glanced in the mirror—and spun around in alarm.

"What do you think you're doing?! You insolent—get off her this instant!"

He lunged an arm toward the back seat, but the Rolls-Royce was wide. Wide enough that the back seat was well out of easy reach. Harry smiled unpleasantly.

"Four seats. Five people. What's the principle—the Pigeonhole Principle, is it? By that logic, at least one seat must hold two people. It's a bit uncomfortable, but I'll manage."

Harry pressed his face into Carina's hair in deliberate provocation, lips curling as though he were about to do something entirely inappropriate at any moment. Lucius, incensed beyond restraint, actually drew his wand.

"You shameless wretch! Get out this instant!"

"Oh? You're pointing a wand with your own daughter sitting right there? Go ahead and try."

"Carina—get away from him! I'll show this idiot what he deserves!"

It was childish. The problem was that when these two were together, it seemed it would never end. Carina pressed her fingers to her throbbing head and addressed them each in turn.

"Father, please stop and put your wand away. Harry, hands off your wand."

But Harry did not stand down. He pulled Carina sideways, out of the wand's line of fire, while his right hand moved to the wand in his pocket—ready to draw at a moment's notice.

Carina knew Harry had access to Voldemort's power. He wouldn't use it against Lucius, of course—but no one is entirely calm in the presence of a loaded weapon. Carina placed a hand on Harry's shoulder.

"Harry."

"I know, Carina. He can't actually hurt me. But what if that idiot fires a spell and it hits you instead?"

"It's my request. Please—just this once, can't you listen?"

When had Carina Malfoy ever sounded this desperate? Watching her plead with an urgency he had rarely seen from her, Harry decided to hold back. He pushed his wand deep into his pocket, exhaled, and released her so she could move.

However much Harry hated to lose, there was no real need to fight tooth and nail against his girlfriend's father. In the end, Harry relented.

"Fine. All right. I'll Apparate somewhere nearby and wait."

"I don't want that."

"What?"

Carina turned sharply. Then she wrapped both arms around Harry's neck and pulled him close. They were pressed together more tightly than before—had their clothes been in any state of disarray, one might have mistaken the scene for something else entirely.
Harry was caught off guard. But Carina's embarrassment ran deeper still. Harry could see the tips of her ears had gone red. Suppressing her mortification with visible effort, Carina looked toward Lucius and said crisply:

"Father. Drive."

Whether it was defiance or something closer to recklessness—either way, Lucius had just witnessed his beloved daughter embrace another man as though she adored him, and he could only open and close his mouth in silence.

"K-Carina? What on earth—"

He must have been waiting for her to laugh it off, to say it was a joke and sit up straight. Surely his daughter—so composed, so well-bred—would never behave like this. Something had to be wrong, Lucius told himself.

But Carina only smiled more coolly and tightened her hold on Harry. They were close enough now to feel each other's heartbeat.

"Whatever you say, Father—I'll be with Harry in the end. If you want to leave us behind, you'll have to leave us both."

"Wha—what—!"

It was not like Carina at all—this open, unguarded display of affection. It landed on Lucius like a blow. But regardless: Carina was swallowing her embarrassment and throwing her weight behind Harry, deliberately.

The girl in his arms was running slightly warm. She was performing composure, but her ears had gone deeply red. The heartbeat he could feel through the press of her chest was loud and fast enough for him to hear.

If Carina was going to this much effort, Harry had to meet her halfway. He shifted his knee slightly, drawing her closer. Lucius's eyes went wider still.

"Think carefully, Mr. Malfoy. Leave us alone together long enough and you might find yourself with three grandchildren to deal with. Stop fighting the inevitable and accept reality."

"You—you—!"

Just as the tension was cresting, the passenger seat, which had been silent until now, spoke in a gentle voice.

"I'm sorry, children—but I don't think there's any need for all this."

"Mum! When are we leaving?"

Two voices rang out from the passenger seat simultaneously. Carina and Harry—and Lucius too—all turned toward the front.

There sat Narcissa, with Draco settled comfortably in her lap.

The others' eyes drifted slowly to the remaining seat. It sat empty, waiting.

Narcissa said, in the tone of someone explaining something obvious:

"The Extension Charm on the front seat is still active. There's plenty of room for two. I'll sit with Draco—so the two of you can sit together in the back."

"Oh… ah…."

Draco, who ordinarily shared the back seat with Carina, was now sitting with Narcissa—which naturally left the back seat free.

Come to think of it, Draco had been conspicuously absent from his usual spot. It seemed Narcissa had moved him to the front on purpose, to give Harry and Carina some space. And yet Harry, Carina, and Lucius had been squabbling childishly over a seat that was never actually taken.

The colour flooded into Carina's face all at once. Without a word, she straightened up and moved quietly to the empty seat.

Lucius, too, had nothing left to say. He faced forward. Draco tugged at his sleeve.

"Dad! When are we leaving?"

"We're going now…."

After all that, the car finally set off. The plush seat of the Rolls-Royce cradled Harry's back. He whistled softly, taking in the interior, and then glanced at the seat beside him. Carina had turned entirely away, not so much as a sliver of her face visible, her gaze fixed on the scenery beyond the window.

What Carina had not considered, however, was that her reflection was perfectly visible in the glass. Harry settled back at his ease and watched her—the faint embarrassment still written across her face. The long, slow exhale. The hand that drifted to her cheek. The small, private fanning of her fingers against the warmth there.

She was unbearably endearing. Harry let his hand drift, slowly, toward her.

Removing the Extension Charm had been a fine idea, as it turned out—it meant he only had to reach out to touch her. He tapped the back of her hand lightly. Carina's gaze, fixed on the window, flickered briefly to him. She took in his playful expression and the finger tapping against her hand, gave a small, disdainful huff—and then, slowly, turned her hand over.

The pale hand that had shown its back now offered its palm to the ceiling. As if asking to be held.

Harry smiled and took it. Their hands met across the gap between the seats. They curled their fingers together, traced idle patterns on each other's knuckles, and for most of the journey simply held on. That was enough.

At some point, Harry tugged Carina's arm toward him. He leaned across and pressed a brief kiss to her lips.

It could not have been forced. The distance between them was too great to bridge without both of them moving toward each other. Across the wide interior of the Rolls-Royce, their faces met—and parted.

That was when Lucius, who had been watching the rear-view mirror, whipped around and glared into the back seat.

"You two—!"

"Oh, caught!"

Like a couple ambushed by a photographer—or rather, exactly like that—both of them froze. Harry pointed urgently toward the front.

"Never mind that, Mr. Malfoy—eyes on the road!"

"This car drives itself! I'm not doing the driving—! More to the point, what do you think you're doing in my car?!"

"What does it look like?"

Harry drew the back of his hand across his mouth with an air of quiet satisfaction.

"Sampling your daughter in your car."

"You—you—! You graceless orphan! Take your hands off her this instant!"

"Ah—that's a bit much, isn't it. Did you contribute to Voldemort killing my parents? Oh, right. You did."

At the name Voldemort, Lucius flinched—and Harry seized the moment, dropping his voice to a low, unhurried drawl.

"Lucius…. Yes, I have no family. And so I cannot fully imagine the grief of losing one. But you would know, wouldn't you…? I trust you will work hard—for the sake of your family…."

"Wh—wheeze—!"

"I told you to stop doing that!"

"Ow! All right, Carina! Sorry!"

The Rolls-Royce hurtling down the road cut a rather erratic figure. The saving grace was that it was a Rolls-Royce—no other vehicle dared come near it—and so, unsteadily but surely, it made its way toward Malfoy Manor.
