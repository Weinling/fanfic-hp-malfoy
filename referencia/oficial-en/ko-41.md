# Traducción inglesa oficial — coreano 41: Moody's Trunk

"I'm going to stick bubblegum to my hair!"

Carina said it, and a brief silence fell.

The first to move was Harry Potter.

"Not the bubblegum!"

Harry flung his wand away with the expression of a man watching the world collapse. The wand spun through the air in lazy rotations and dropped into the trunk. Moody caught it on reflex.

Moody stared at Harry's wand with profound disappointment. A low voice rumbled out of him.

"What was that, Potter? Did you just throw your wand?"

"Carina, stay still! Don't touch your hair!"

Harry ignored Moody entirely and shouted urgently toward Carina. Moody's face twisted into a deep scowl as he raised Harry's wand.

"...That was a genuinely stupid thing to do, Potter. If you want to protect the person you love, the one thing you must never let go of—above all else—is your wand. Without it, you can accomplish nothing!"

Moody leveled the wand slowly at Carina.

What he held was Harry's wand. That wand was aimed at Carina. Threatening, as though a curse might burst from it at any moment.

"A wand taken from you becomes your enemy's weapon instantly! Remember that!"

To condemn Harry's foolishness in releasing his wand so carelessly, to teach him a lesson—that was why Moody intended to curse Carina with it.

Carina, who hadn't anticipated he would go quite this far, widened her eyes and stared at the wand pointed at her.

And then she noticed something.

'Wait. That wand...?'

What Moody was aiming wasn't Harry's wand.

Harry's wand was holly, eleven inches, purchased at Ollivander's—supple and impressively flexible when swung.

But the wand in Moody's hand was shorter, darker, and looked far shoddier.

Like a toy someone had made.

A toy?

"*Incarcerous*!"

Moody cast the spell. The instant that thought crossed her mind, Carina swung her own wand in answer.

"*Expelliarmus*!"

Moody had clearly spoken first—but what left his wand was not a binding spell.

With a loud pop, the trick wand in his hand transformed into a rubber snake. With a sharp hiss, the snake whipped its head around and turned on Moody instead.

Moody hurled the toy snake away and immediately lunged for his real wand. But Carina's magic was faster. Her Disarming Charm tore the wand from his grip.

Carina snatched the wand out of the air and leveled both—hers and Moody's—at him.

"...That one's a fake. It seems even your eye can't tell a real wand from an imitation, Professor."

"Caught me there..."

Moody said it slowly. On the surface he looked deflated, disarmed and at a loss—but Carina didn't miss the way his eye swiveled toward something across the room.

Mad-Eye Moody was unhinged. He'd been delighted as a child when she started suspecting him, and the moment a fight broke out he'd seized his wand with the relish of a man who lived for real combat.

A man who lived up to the name Mad-Eye was not the sort to give up over a lost wand.

She couldn't afford to drop her guard. Carina cast immediately.

"*Petrificus Totalus*!"

Two beams of light burst from her wand. One went wide, but the other struck Moody squarely in the chest.

He went rigid and toppled backward. A small marble of some kind rolled from his hand as he fell.
He must have struck his head on the way down—a thin line of blood traced its way across his temple.

Rita Skeeter screamed.

"Eek! Murder!"

"He's not dead! He's only been hit with a Body-Bind!"

Carina steadied her trembling chest and looked down at Moody.

It was the first time she had ever cast a spell on a professor. If she had been even slightly slower with the Body-Bind, there was no telling what outlandish method Moody might have tried next.

So her actions were right. There was nothing wrong with how she had handled it.

Carina told herself that, quietly, firmly.

"Carina. Well done! It was half a gamble, but I'm glad it worked out!"

Harry came leaping down the ladder. He pulled the fake trick wand from Moody's hand as he spoke.

"It's one of Fred and George's Fake Wands. How did you know?"

"I didn't."

Carina kept her wand trained on Moody as she answered.

"I only knew that wasn't your wand. I remember what yours looks like..."

She hadn't meant anything particular by it. But Harry smiled with unmistakable satisfaction and wrapped his arms around her from behind.

Carina startled and cried out.

"W-wait, stop! This isn't the time for that!"

"Can't help it when you're being this adorable."

"What exactly did I do that was so—!"

"And the threat you came up with was bubblegum. Where did you learn to say something so terrifying? I thought my heart was going to give out."

"Ugh...!"

Sirius, meanwhile, had forcibly turned his head away from the two of them.

"Having a grand time, are we... Right. I suppose the rest falls to the godfather."

Sirius subdued Rita Skeeter, who was still seated at the desk, with brisk efficiency. Even as the ropes wound tight around her, she kept shouting.

"I know who you are! You're Sirius Black—the notorious criminal Dumbledore sprung through a plea deal! You can tie me up all you like, but the truth always comes out in the end!"

"Has the tabloid hack finally lost the plot entirely? Since when are you on the side of justice?"

With the matter settled, Sirius noticed the papers lying on the desk. He furrowed his handsome brow and read through the documents with intense focus.

The volume of information was considerable, and it took him quite a long time to work through even a rough overview. By the time Carina had finally managed to extricate herself from Harry's arms, Sirius murmured with a grave expression.

"...Malfoy. Did you know about this? Is that why you told us to stop fighting?"

As he spoke, Sirius held the documents out for Carina to see.

Investigation files on Karkaroff. On Ludo Bagman. On Olympe Maxime.

And thinner ones—but present nonetheless—on Dumbledore and Barty Crouch Sr.

Everything Moody had compiled over the past several months on everyone connected to the Triwizard Tournament was there. Past records, suspicious details—all of it.

Carina nodded.

"Yes."

Moody remained deeply suspicious. His behavior, his words—everything about him was far removed from any ordinary person.

But his purpose—the goal he was circling toward, however indirectly—was becoming clear.

Carina looked down at Moody and spoke.
"He might be the real Moody."

Sirius levitated Moody and Rita Skeeter with a spell and climbed the ladder. Carina followed, and Harry came last.

Climbing a ladder up through the mouth of a trunk was a genuinely peculiar experience. Sirius disappeared, and a rectangle of sky came into view.

The ladder ended at an awkward height. Standing on the last rung, Carina reached up and gripped the edge of the trunk—but the rim was narrow, and the metal bit into her palms.

Then Sirius extended his hand.

"Here. Take it."

Carina accepted the help without hesitation. Sirius hauled her up in one clean motion. It felt rather like being a Mandrake torn from its pot—she came up all at once, in a single swift pull.

The cool evening air washed over her. She felt, with sudden and genuine appreciation, how fresh and precious a simple breeze could be.

Perhaps because this was her first time being kidnapped—she had been so tense that her back was soaked through with cold sweat. Setting aside the discomfort, the thought that Harry had pressed himself against that same damp back not long ago made her feel distinctly unsettled.

She had been trapped for less than an hour, but it felt like a full day had passed. Every bit of strength had drained out of her.

"Honestly, Sirius. Don't go grabbing other people's girlfriends."

Harry grumbled as he hauled himself out of the trunk. Sirius's long-suffering sigh carried clearly across the distance.

"Now you're keeping tabs on your godfather too?"

"I'm not keeping tabs on you. It's a matter of principle. Men above a certain threshold of attractiveness are not permitted to come near her."

The shamelessness of it was too much. Carina, unable to contain her embarrassment, rounded on Harry.

"Idiot. What are you saying the moment we get out?"

"I'm anxious. What if you get kidnapped again like this?"

Harry answered with breezy nonchalance and moved naturally to Carina's side. Every motion flowed seamlessly into the next—until he took her hand and pressed his lips to her ring.

"Good thing I check the ring constantly. What would I have done otherwise?"

The ring glowed pink, still shining in Carina's hand. Harry looked at it for a moment, then drew her arm toward him and moved to pull her into an embrace.

Carina pushed him back quickly.

"Wait, Harry. Don't come any closer."

"Why? Do you not want me to?"

"No, it's not that."

She looked at his crestfallen expression, then glanced away and said quietly,

"I haven't been able to freshen up. I was tied up, and I sweated a little..."

"All the better."

"What? No—wait. That's absolutely—!"

Carina barely managed to push Harry away as he tried to bury his face against her neck. She glared at him with flushed cheeks. Harry was still smiling—the kind of smile that made people feel, against all reason, that everything was going to be fine.

Carina's expression softened for just a moment.

Being cherished and loved felt genuinely sweet. Harry, who came to her without caring about sweat or anything else, was both her greatest pride and her love. There were times even Carina thought she didn't deserve it—that it was more than she had any right to.

But Moody's words would not leave her mind.

What if this feeling became a weapon used to hurt Harry? What if, someday, this emotion and this bond became the very thing that closed around both their throats?

If that day came—could Harry endure it? Could she?

"Dumbledore."

It was then. At Sirius's murmur, both Harry and Carina turned.

Along the lakeshore—with water and earth as his backdrop—Dumbledore was walking slowly toward them.
