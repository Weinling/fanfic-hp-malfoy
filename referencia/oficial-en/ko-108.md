So many of you have supported this story over the course of its run. I never wrote it with money in mind, so when the support actually started coming in, I was genuinely caught off guard. I'd half-planned to write a bit and then quietly disappear... but getting hit with actual cash has a way of snapping you back to your senses.

At the time there was no refund button, so I spent a while fretting over what to do—and eventually decided that having accepted the money, I owed it to everyone to see things through. I sat back down at the keyboard. I picked out the parts of the source material I actually liked, built a skeleton, and fleshed it out into a story. Books three and four were popular and tightly constructed to begin with, so those went well enough—but with books five and six, I tried to trim the frustrating bits and somehow ended up carving out even more of the dry, stringy parts.

Anyway. Pathetically or not, I did manage to drag the story to its end.

You might not like how it ended. Or perhaps you jumped ship long before the ending ever arrived. Some of you might be thinking: I sponsored this thing and it grew up into this garbage? Ugh, disgusting. I know. I know some of you are thinking exactly that—sob—yes, I know you are!
For those people: Gosokdoru's Gringotts is now open!

If you sponsored the story but dropped it midway because the content wasn't to your liking—or if you stayed until the end but the ending left you cold—I'm holding a sponsorship refund event. Just leave a comment!

What's that? You're saying those people already left and won't even see this notice?
You're right. This is the legendary "raise your hand if you're not here!" in the flesh. A monster spawned by capitalism, quietly hoarding its safe assets.
